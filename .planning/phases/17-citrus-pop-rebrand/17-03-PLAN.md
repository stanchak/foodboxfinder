# Plan 17-03: Pill Buttons and Rounded Radii

## Objective

Update all interactive elements (buttons, inputs, CTAs, pagination) to use pill-shaped borders (rounded-full) and increase card radii to match the Citrus Pop playful aesthetic. Depends on Plan 17-01 (CSS tokens) being complete.

In src/components/Button.tsx:
- Change `rounded-lg` → `rounded-full` in the base button className
